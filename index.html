Account & Data Deletion Request

App: Tars Money Manager

How to Delete Your Account & Data

If you wish to delete your Google Sign-In account and all associated data from Tars Money Manager, please follow these steps:

  1. Open the Tars Money Manager app on your device.
  2. Go to Settings > Account.
  3. Click on "Delete Account" or "Clear All Data".

Alternatively, you can send an email request to us at: tarsmoneymanager@gmail.com with the subject "Account Deletion Request" from your registered Google email address.

What data will be deleted?

  • Your Google login authentication session.
  • All locally stored income, expense, and transaction logs.

Note: Data is deleted instantly upon clicking "Delete Account" inside the app or within 2 business days via email request.
